Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before subscribing.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a student discount?

Absolutely. Students with a valid .edu email get Pro for $4/month. Contact support to verify.

What happens to my progress if I cancel?

Your progress is saved forever on the free tier. You won't lose anything — you'll just lose access to premium features.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.
